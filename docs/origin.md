# Origin

The kit comes out of the Minion-Agent retrospective of Layers 11–14 and the Minion-Memory adoption:

- Record: `EGAILab/minion-agent-docs` `process/2026-10-11-reusable-workflow-retrospective.md`
  (revision 3 at `752bbb14`). Its §6 is the kit's design; §7 lists the Owner decisions; §8 the phases.
- Owner decisions (verbatim transcripts): `EGAILab/minion-agent#205`.
- P1 coordination: `EGAILab/minion-agent#211`.
- Seed commit of the process text and tool: `EGAILab/minion-agent-docs` `dd9fd15188d89597fd09a538c2652788604d0d92`.

The Minion-Agent-specific rules the kit does not carry (the pinned Pi baseline, the practical-parity
Owner decision, the Minion-Agent repositories, panes and gate commands) live in Minion-Agent's profile
and its own docs. Historical review records, proposals and friction analyses stay in
`minion-agent-docs/process/` and `assurance/`; the lessons extracted from them are in `lessons/`.
